#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Several KPIs provide insights into JetBlue's operational efficiency and revenue generation:

*   **Available Seat Miles (ASM):** Measures the airline's capacity. In 2024, system ASMs decreased by 3.5% compared to 2023 [Source: None].
*   **Revenue Passenger Miles (RPM):** Measures the number of miles flown by revenue passengers.
*   **Load Factor:** The percentage of aircraft seating capacity utilized. In the third quarter of 2024, JetBlue's load factor was 86.6%, compared to 85.1% in the same period in 2023 [Source: None].
*   **Passenger Revenue per ASM (cents):** A measure of passenger revenue generated per available seat mile. In the third quarter of 2024, passenger revenue per ASM was 13.13 cents, compared to 12.68 cents in the same period in 2023 [Source: None].
*   **Operating Revenue per ASM (cents):** A measure of operating revenue generated per available seat mile. In the third quarter of 2024, operating revenue per ASM was 14.13 cents, compared to 13.55 cents in the same period in 2023 [Source: None].
*   **CASM ex-fuel:** A key metric for airline cost management. In 2024, CASM ex-fuel increased by 6.6% to 10.55 cents year-over-year [Source: None].
